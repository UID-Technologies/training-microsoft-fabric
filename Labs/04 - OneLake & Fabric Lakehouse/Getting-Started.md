# Getting started — Module 4 (OneLake & Lakehouse)

1. Read **[Labs/Getting-Started.md](../Getting-Started.md)** (accounts, Fabric capacity).  
2. Open **[README.md](./README.md)** and choose Lab 01 or Lab 02.  
3. Follow that lab’s **`.md` guide top to bottom** — upload files from **that lab’s `data/` folder only**.  
4. Replace `<id>` in workspace names with your initials (example: `M4_L01_js`).  
5. UI help: **[Navigation reference](../_shared/Fabric-and-Power-BI-Navigation.md)**.

Labs are **standalone** (separate workspace and lakehouse each). Lab 02 does not depend on Lab 01 in the tenant.
