# Module 1 — Introduction to Data Analytics & Business Intelligence

Hands-on labs for learners building CRM analytics skills in **Microsoft Fabric**. All currency amounts are fictional (INR).

## Start here (learners)

1. Read **[Getting-Started.md](./Getting-Started.md)** — accounts, capacity, and how to finish any lab end to end.  
2. Open **one** lab guide below and follow it from top to bottom.  
3. Fill in **[Learner_Worksheet.md](./Learner_Worksheet.md)** as you go (submit per your instructor).  

Each lab is **standalone** (its own workspace, lakehouse, and `data/` folder). You do not need Power BI Desktop.

## Topics you will practice

- Operational vs analytical work  
- Structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data  
- Dimensions, measures, grain, and relationships  
- Data quality and impact on joins  
- KPI definitions, targets, and honest interpretation
